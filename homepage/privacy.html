Privacy & analytics

I use analytics to learn which pages visitors explore, what works, and what needs repair. The public Living Universe can be explored without signing in.

By default, the site counts anonymous pageviews and specifically labeled interactions without saving a browser identifier between visits. You can opt into recognizing this browser on return, or stop analytics entirely, below. This may not perfectly distinguish individual people or devices.

PostHog may receive technical browser/device information and infer approximate location. Session replay, broad automatic click capture, and collection of form contents are disabled. We do not intentionally send family schedules, addresses, private itinerary details, messages, or saved exploration files to analytics. A site's analytics provider may process connection IPs; these choices do not guarantee total anonymity.
