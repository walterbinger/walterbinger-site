# Life of Pie — Pizza Party Capture Guide

Status: working field guide for new source material.

## Minimum capture set per pie

1. **Whole pie / top-down**
   - Get the entire crust inside frame.
   - Leave clean breathing room around the pie.
   - Best source for a satellite sticker.

2. **Whole pie / slight angle**
   - 20–40° off horizontal.
   - Show crust height, char, and topping relief.
   - Best source for archive/beauty use.

3. **Pie with one slice removed**
   - Keep the remaining pie visible.
   - Creates a useful chapter/fragment composition.

4. **Single slice**
   - Shoot it alone on a simple surface if possible.
   - Get at least one profile that clearly shows crust and underside.
   - Best source for slice stickers.

5. **One detail**
   - Crust blister, underside char, sauce/cheese edge, or unusual topping detail.
   - Use for texture and process layers.

## Sticker-friendly shooting rules

- Prefer a plain or visually quiet background.
- Keep the full pie/slice unobstructed; no hands crossing the silhouette if avoidable.
- Do not crop the crust edge.
- Avoid hard shadows crossing the pie when possible.
- One clean source frame is more valuable than ten busy beauty shots.
- Shoot at the highest native phone resolution; do not use screenshots or social-app exports as the master.
- Preserve the original file. Crops/edits belong downstream.

## Party-specific opportunistic shots

- Multiple finished pies together before serving.
- A row of pies at different stages.
- One overhead table shot after the spread is complete.
- First slice being lifted from a pie.
- Empty/half-eaten aftermath if it tells the story.
- Hands reaching in can be great archive material, but also capture a clean unobstructed version first.

## Naming convention

Use:
`YYYY-MM-DD - Pizza - [Short Name] - [View] - ORIGINAL.ext`

Examples:
- `2026-10-04 - Pizza - Mushroom Bacon - Whole Top - ORIGINAL.heic`
- `2026-10-04 - Pizza - Mushroom Bacon - Slice Profile - ORIGINAL.heic`
- `2026-10-04 - Pizza - Grandma - Whole Angle - ORIGINAL.heic`

Do not force the final pie title at capture time. A plain descriptive working name is enough.

## Core rule

**World = cutout object. Archive = full photograph.**

Take both kinds of source material on purpose.
