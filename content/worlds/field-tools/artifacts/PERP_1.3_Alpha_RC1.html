3. Use the repair tools.

These are the sticky pieces. They work in meetings, debriefs, conflict, operations, and relationships.

Meeting Reset

Use when a conversation is spinning.

  • What decision are we making?
  • What evidence do we have?
  • What are we assuming?
  • What repair are we choosing?
  • What proof will we check?

Incident Card

Use after something goes wrong.

  • What happened?
  • What pressure was present?
  • What evidence was missing?
  • What repair is needed?
  • What proof confirms change?

Conflict Debrief

Use when people are blaming each other.

  • What did each person think was happening?
  • Where did interpretation outrun evidence?
  • What needs repair: trust, role, process, or capacity?

Proof Check

Use after the fix.

  • Owner?
  • Date?
  • Observable signal?
  • What changed?
  • What still repeats?
