Throw it from OFF to ON, or scrub the angle. The handle and both copper blades share one hinge axis. The porcelain insulators, busbars, lugs and contact jaws do not move.

Two cables. One circuit.

IN: A heavy insulated braided cable ends in a bolted copper lug on the rear busbar, which feeds both hinge terminals.

OUT: A matching cable terminates on the front busbar, linking both fixed jaws. Two blades close the same electrical path in parallel.

Render and animation are based on one set of computed 3D coordinates; this is a proof of motion and construction, not a validated high-current electrical design.
