Treat these as explicit **REJECTED MECHANICAL GEOMETRY** examples. **No further whole-switch image-to-image redraws.** First image is a reference for copper, iron, Bakelite, aged-paper illustration and patina only; second is evidence of geometry drift. Freeze engineering geometry in procedural SVG/Blender/mesh, rig one common hinge and check OFF/ON with exactly two heavy cables. Gemini/Nano Banana may generate isolated material textures, engraving motifs, patina treatments, lighting concepts and backgrounds; apply them to frozen material slots without altering the silhouette or adding parts. A technical reviewer must compare actual 3D meshes/coordinates rather than trusting an illustrative multiview sheet. This correction supersedes older instructions inviting Nano Banana to redraw full switch geometry.

## Core rule
One creative owner (Walter), one canonical design contract (Scout), one integration repository (Codex/GitHub), and deliberately *competing* specialist outputs where comparison is worthwhile. A fantastic-looking render is evidence of aesthetic direction, not functioning geometry. Keep all source/editable assets.

## Specific AI jobs
CHATGPT / SCOUT (here): producer and historian. Source of task briefs, acceptance evidence, approvals and Drive/GitHub continuity. Does NOT pretend to be the illustration specialist.
CLAUDE: cinematic/industrial/art-critical and editorial Red Team. It should never be the sole reviewer of its own code/art.
CODEX: builder of geometry scripts, animation skeletons, field solver, audio runtime, Three.js projection, modular integration, cross-platform checks. It has practical browser/local-code workflows.
NANO BANANA PRO: reference-faithful illustration, style lock, multi-image reference composition, texture/layout variants, and production paint-overs of approved 3D renders. Does not replace editable geometry.
MIDJOURNEY V8.2: optional independent look-development challenger when the composition seems uninspired. Use only to beat or enrich Nano Banana work, NOT a second mandatory image subscription.
MESHY: optional generation of small NONcritical static 3D assets. Current features support multi-view images, GLB/BLEND output and PBR texture maps. Mechanical parts with exact physics better created parametrically.
TRIPO: optional head-to-head competitor to Meshy only if difficult segmentation or geometry requires it. Never pay for both before a side-by-side small-asset trial.
BLENDER: free open-source 3D modeling/animation, not an AI itself. Use Codex to write Blender Python that builds hinged physical parts and reliable ports.
THREE.JS: browser WebGL renderer, not an AI itself. Accepts web GLB exports with GLTFLoader. Projector should consume solved physics, not generated decorative loops.
TONE.JS / WEB AUDIO: browser synthesis/sampler/spatial audio library/runtime, not an AI by itself. Licensed/made-for-project soft timbres (velvet keys, tube organ, softened glass, marimba) and mechanical transient sound. No requirement to buy music-generation SaaS.
WALTER: always the final judge of joy, authenticity, comfort and publish-readiness.

## Spending discipline
Tier 0 (start now): existing ChatGPT/Codex + Claude plans, Blender free, Three.js, Tone.js and repo/Drive. Build geometry with scripts; borrow no third-party paid tool until a clear gate needs it.
Tier 1 (only for lookdev): Nano Banana Pro for coherent concept/paintover passes. Check currently accessible/free credits first. One reference-approved assembly plate, not dozens of render lotteries.
Tier 2 (only after structured test): Meshy OR Tripo for static asset conversion, and optionally Midjourney V8.2 if its independently generated illustration outperforms Nano Banana. Don't buy overlapping monthly plans to evaluate a single part.
AI-generated art and assets must have licensing suitable for a public professional website. Preserve edit source, commercial use permissions and credits/attribution obligations (e.g., free-tier Meshy license where applicable).

## The first exact cross-model handoff
WALTER approves physical switch behavior against the user's supplied photos.
SCOUT packages mechanical constraints, orthographic 2-view request, materials, unchanging camera and endpoint map.
NANO BANANA PRO proposes two coherent render directions (not mechanism design); MIDJOURNEY optional third direction.
CLAUDE writes a short reject/approve review focused on hinge reality, matching front and side views, real sockets, proper camera.
CODEX constructs editable Blender geometry with named pivots, three contact positions and tests actual top-to-bottom throw; capture OFF and ON screenshots.
WALTER chooses KEEP / CHANGE / DISCARD.
Only then integrate switch into assembly and proceed to sound and projection.

## Claim boundary
Physically-inspired wave simulations are not experimentally validated cymatics. Color/material generation is not production geometry. Website audio quality has to be evaluated by ears. '4D' is visual shorthand for time-evolving 3D state—not literal depiction of four spatial axes.
