### Specialist art brief starter
Produce a coherent multi-angle physically operable experimental apparatus, not a standalone poster. Show exposed twin copper knife arms on hinged center with black grip traveling UP OFF → DOWN ON into contact jaws. Include sockets/ferrules/ports and power flow through connected cable harness; engraved ink crosshatching and warm aged brass, copper, soot, iron, cream porcelain, Bakelite and subdued colored glass. Front orthographic plus three-quarter view MUST match in construction; lighting and camera from one master scene. Provide component-separated layers with pivot coordinates, z-order, socket identifiers and source-editable files. Show workbench and projector while playing. NO brand name, no floating cables, no invented device copy, no film-asset reproduction. Compare reference image and moving proof; don't claim mechanics are sound without the animation test.

### Audio lab starter
Build and render 15s examples of warm velvet keys, softened glass, tube organ and marimba using the very same continuous InstrumentState and WaveFrame parameters as the visualizer. Move X/Y slowly, engage injector ratios, capture 3 states. Preserve smooth pitch transitions, occasional soft centering clicks and audible harmonic increase, but avoid sustained abrasive drones or startling peak gain. Provide reproducible code/audio and a 60s idle comfort check.

### Wave / projector lab starter
Reuse the V12 damped membrane model as candidate, state its approximations, and prove frame/phase/partial changes map to the same sound controls. Demonstrate at least five widely separated parameter states, two very close states, superposition of captured layers, strong/weak resonance, reduced-motion fallback, and 390px/mobile frame time. The cognitive atlas overlay is admitted to be artistic, not anatomical science.
